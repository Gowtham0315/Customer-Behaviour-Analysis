# 📊 Customer Behaviour Dashboard

A complete end-to-end Data Analytics project that analyzes customer purchasing behavior using **SQL**, **Power BI**, and **PostgreSQL**. The project focuses on customer demographics, purchasing patterns, subscription behavior, product performance, and revenue insights through interactive dashboards.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Businesses collect large amounts of customer transaction data but often struggle to convert it into meaningful business insights. This project analyzes customer purchase data to understand customer behavior, spending patterns, subscription trends, and product performance.

The analysis was performed using SQL for data extraction and business analysis, while Power BI was used to build an interactive dashboard for visualization.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Retail businesses need to understand customer purchasing behavior to improve revenue, customer retention, and product performance. However, raw transactional data alone cannot provide actionable insights.

This project transforms customer purchase data into meaningful business intelligence by analyzing:

- Customer demographics
- Revenue contribution
- Subscription behavior
- Product performance
- Sales distribution
- Customer segments
- Review ratings

## 📈 SQL Business Questions

The project answers the following business questions:

1. Total revenue generated by male vs female customers.
2. Customers who used discounts but spent above the average purchase amount.
3. Top 5 highest-rated products.
4. Average purchase amount by shipping type.
5. Spending comparison between subscribed and non-subscribed customers.
6. Products with the highest percentage of discounted purchases.
7. Customer segmentation based on previous purchases.
8. Top 3 purchased products within each category.
9. Subscription behavior of repeat buyers.
10. Revenue contribution by age group.

## 📊 Dashboard Insights

### Customer Overview

- Total Customers: **3.9K**
- Average Purchase Amount: **$59.76**
- Average Review Rating: **3.75**

### Subscription Insights

- 73% of customers are non-subscribers.
- Only 27% of customers have active subscriptions.

### Category Performance

- Clothing generates the highest revenue and sales.
- Accessories rank second in both revenue and sales.
- Outerwear contributes the least revenue.

### Age Group Analysis

- Young Adults contribute the highest revenue.
- Young Adults also generate the highest sales volume.
- Adult and Senior customers contribute almost equally.

### Business Observation

The business depends heavily on non-subsscribed customers, indicating an opportunity to increase subscription conversions through targeted promotions and loyalty programs.
